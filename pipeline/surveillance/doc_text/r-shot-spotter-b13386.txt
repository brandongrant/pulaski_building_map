3 A RESOLUTION TO AUTHORIZE THE CITY MANAGER TO EXECUTE
4 A TWO (2)-YEAR CONTRACT WITH SHOTSPOTTER, INC., (D/B/A SST,
5 INC.), IN A TOTAL AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED TWO HUNDRED
6 NINETY DOLLARS ($290,000.00), FOR THE PURCHASE OF A WIDE
7 AREA GUNSHOT DETECTION SERVICE FOR THE LITTLE ROCK
8 POLICE DEPARTMENT; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
9
10 WHEREAS, since September 11, 2001, the City has become eligible for several Emergency Service
11 Equipment Grants; and
12 WHEREAS, ShotSpotter, Inc., (d/b/a SST, Inc.) (“ShotSpotter”) operates on the principle of
13 multilateration, based upon the precise time the sound of gunfire arrives at each sensor utilizing audio
14 sensors installed on top of buildings or on poles in the designated service area; and
15 WHEREAS, SST, Inc., is the exclusive provider for ShotSpotter Flex, the cloud-based, subscription
16 managed service offering, which includes warranty, maintenance and support services on its system; and
17 WHEREAS, multiple sensors pick up on the gun shot's sound and transmits data to the SST cloud-
18 based server, where the company's proprietary software analyzes the data to classify the incident and
19 determine the exact location, transmits the data to the SST “Incident Review Center” within seconds an
20 SST professional reviewer analyzes data and recordings to confirm gunfire before the alert of shots fired,
21 map location and audio data of the shooting is sent directly to the City’s Police Dispatch Workstations and
22 Patrol Officers in-vehicle computers and smartphones; and
23 WHEREAS, it is critical that the Little Rock Police Department be equipped and trained with reliable
24 forms of response intervention equipment to preserve public safety; and,
25 WHEREAS, the contract amount of Two Hundred Ninety Thousand Dollars ($290,000.00), which
26 includes the one (1)-time service startup fees, onboarding, training and best practices and a two (2)-year
27 subscription fee to cover a two (2) square-mile area will be paid by a Department of Justice FY 17
28 Technology Innovation for Public Safety (TIPS) Grant in the amount of Two Hundred Fifty Thousand
29 Dollars ($250,000.00), Account No. 270529-G35158, and the remaining Forty Thousand Dollars
30 ($40,000.00) balance will be paid from the Federal Equitable Sharing Seized Funds, Account No. 270529-
31 G35148;
32 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE CITY
33 OF LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS:
[Page 1 of 2]

13 resolution.

15 the provisions of this resolution, are hereby repealed to the extent of such inconsistency.

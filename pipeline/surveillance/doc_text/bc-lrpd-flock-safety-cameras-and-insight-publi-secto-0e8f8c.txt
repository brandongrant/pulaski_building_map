SYNOPSIS A resolution to amend Little Rock, Ark. Resolution No.
16,489 (November 4, 2024), to authorize the City Manager
to enter into a contract with Insight Public Sector, Inc., for
a one (1)-year subscription renewal for the Little Rock
Police Department Real Time Crime Center.
FISCAL IMPACT The total cost for this renewal is $77,500.00. This will be
paid from Account No. 105225-63360.
RECOMMENDATION Approval of the resolution.
BACKGROUND Flock Safety has notified the department of a group of
cameras that need a contract subscription renewal. Flock
Safety is the city’s provider for license plate readers
(LPRs) and is an essential tool for the apprehension of
criminals and the prevention of crime. Resolution 16,489
included the renewal cost of $284,500. The amended
amount is $362,000, including the cost of $77,500 to
cover the license plate recognition camera with the
vehicle fingerprint software.

ORDINANCE NO. 93q2 --
AN ORDINANCE WAIVING FORMAL BIDDING REQUIREMENTS AND
AUTHORIZING PAYMENT TO SKYCOP INC., A SOLE SOURCE
VENDOR, FOR THE PURCHASE OF LICENSE PLATE READER
CAMERAS FOR THE NORTH LITTLE ROCK POLICE DEPARTMENT;
DECLARING AN EMERGENCY; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
WHEREAS, Ark. Code Ann. § 14-58-303 requires City purchases exceeding the amount
of $20,000 to follow statutory procedures of local advertisement and opening of sealed bids which
may only be waived in exceptional situations where bidding is deemed not feasible or practical;
and
WHEREAS, the North Little Rock Police Department ("NLRPD") is in need of license
plate reader ("LPR") cameras, which are mounted on poles that capture the numbers and letters of
license plates on still or moving vehicles; and
WHEREAS, all LPR camera vendors require use of their specific cameras in order to be
compatible with their system; and
WHEREAS, the NLRPD uses the City of Little Rock's LPR Server, who uses SkyCop Inc.
as their LPR camera reader; and
WHEREAS, the City Council has previously authorized the waiver of formal bidding
requirements for the purchase of LPR cameras from SkyCop Inc.; and
WHEREAS, the NLRPD is requesting the purchase and installation of seven (7) additional
cameras from SkyCop Inc. at a total price of Sixty Four Thousand Four Hundred Ninety Nine and
88/100 Dollars ($64,499.88) including tax, in order to be compatible with the LRPD's LPR server
(see Quotes from Skycop, Inc. attached collectively hereto as Exhibit A).
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
NORTH LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS:
SECTION 1: That formal bidding is hereby waived in connection with the purchase of
LPR cameras from SkyCop Inc. for a total amount of Sixty Four Thousand Four Hundred Ninety
Nine and 88/100 cents ($64,499.88).
SECTION 2: That the cost of the cameras is included in and shall be paid from the 2021
I.T. Department Budget.
SECTION 3: That all ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby
repealed to the extent of the conflict.
1
SECTION 4: That the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared to be severable,
and if any section, phrase or provision shall be declared or held invalid, such invalidity shall not
affect the remainder of the sections, phrases or provisions.
SECTION 5: That it is hereby found and determined that it is in the best interest of the
City of North Little Rock to purchase said LPR cameras from SkyCop Inc., and is necessary for
the immediate preservation of the public health, safety and welfare; THEREFORE, an emergency
is hereby declared to exist, and this Ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its
passage and approval.

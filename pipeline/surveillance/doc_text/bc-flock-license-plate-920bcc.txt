SYNOPSIS A resolution to authorize the City Manager to enter into a
contract with Insight Public Sector to renew Little Rock
Police Department’s Flock Safety Software Subscription
Services to support Falcon License Plate Cameras installed
throughout the City for two (2) one (1)-year terms, at an
annual rate of $345,000.00 for a total expenditure of
$690,000.00 over the life of the contract.
FISCAL IMPACT The annual renewal cost is $345,00.00 and will be paid from
the department’s software account 105201-61350. Vendor
selection was made through the OMINA Partners #23-6692-
03.
RECOMMENDATION Adoption of the resolution.
BACKGROUND Since 2020, Flock has been our provider for license plate
readers. With this purchase, the department will continue to
have the necessary intelligence, preventive, and investigative
tools to apprehend criminals, assist with the prevention of
locate and locate missing persons. Flock Safety has notified
the department of a group of cameras that need a contract
subscription renewal. Flock Safety is the city’s provider for
license plate readers (LPRs) and is an essential tool for the
apprehension of criminals and the prevention of crime.
Nothing additional is added, current services will remain.

AN ORDINANCE WAIVING FORMAL BIDDING
REQUIREMENTS FOR THE PURCHASE OF LICENSE PLATE
READER CAMERAS FROM SKYCORP INC., A SOLE SOURCE
VENDOR; DECLARING AN EMERGENCY; AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES.
WHEREAS, Ark. Code Ann. § 14-58-303 requires City purchases exceeding the
amount of $20,000 to follow statutory procedures of local advertisement and opening of
sealed bids which may only be waived in exceptional situations where bidding is deemed
not feasible or practical; and
WHEREAS, license plate reader ("LPR") cameras are cameras mounted on poles
that capture the numbers and letters of license plates on a still or moving vehicle; and
WHEREAS, the North Little Rock Police Department ("NLRPD") currently uses
LPR cameras in order to aid in their duties as officers of the law; and
WHERAS, all LPR camera vendors require use of their specific cameras in order
to be compatible with their system; and
WHEREAS, the NLRPD uses the City of Little Rock's LPR Server; and
WHEREAS, the Little Rock Police Department ("LRPD") conducted a request
for proposal bid, and selected SkyCorp Inc., as their LPR camera vendor; and
and
WHEREAS, SkyCorp Inc., is the sole source vendor for compatible LPR cameras;
WHEREAS, the NLRPD is requesting to purchase four (4) cameras from
SkyCorp Inc. at a price of Eight Thousand Three Hundred & 00/100 Dollars ($8,300.00)
each in order to be compatible with the LRPD's LPR server.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF NORTH LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS:
SECTION 1: That formal bidding is hereby waived in connection with the
purchase of LPR cameras from SkyCorp Inc. for a total amount of Thirty -Three Thousand
Two Hundred & 00/100 Dollars ($33,200.00).
SECTION 2: That the sum of Thirty -Three Thousand Two Hundred & 00/100
Dollars ($33,200.00) will be paid from the Police Fund, said amount having been
included in the 2018 Budget.

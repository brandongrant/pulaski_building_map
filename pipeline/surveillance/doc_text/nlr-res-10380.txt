RESOLUTION NO. /19 3U
A RESOLUTION WAIVING FORMAL BIDDING REQUIREMENTS AND
AUTHORIZING PAYMENT TO SKYCOP, INC. FOR THE PURCHASE OF
LICENSE PLATE READER CAMERAS FOR THE NORTH LITTLE ROCK
POLICE DEPARTMENT; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
WHEREAS, Ark. Code Ann. § 14-58-303 requires City purchases exceeding the amount
of $35,000 to follow statutory procedures of local advertisement and opening of sealed bids which
may only be waived in exceptional situations where bidding is deemed not feasible or practical;
and
WHEREAS, the North Little Rock Police Department ("NLRPD") is in need of annual
upgrades and replacements to the City's system of license plate reader ("LPR") cameras, which
are mounted on poles that capture the numbers and letters of license plates on still or moving
vehicles; and
WHEREAS, the NLRPD must use cameras compatible with the current system, which was
previously purchased and installed by SkyCop, Inc., 3736 Getwell Cove, Memphis, Tennessee
38118; and
WHEREAS, the NLRPD is requesting the purchase of upgrades and replacements of five
(5) additional LPR cameras from SkyCop, Inc. for Forty Two Thousand Nine Hundred Twenty -
Five and 00/100 Dollars ($42,925.00) without tax (see Quote from Skycop, Inc. attached hereto as
Exhibit A).
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
NORTH LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS:
SECTION 1: That formal bidding is hereby waived in connection with the purchase of
upgrades and replacements of license plate reader cameras from SkyCop, Inc. for a total amount
of Forty Two Thousand Nine Hundred Twenty Five and 00/100 Dollars ($42,925.00).
SECTION 2: That the cost of the cameras shall be paid from the 2022 IT Department
Budget.
SECTION 3: That the provisions of this Resolution are hereby declared to be severable,
and if any section, phrase or provision shall be declared or held invalid, such invalidity shall not
affect the remainder of the sections, phrases or provisions.
SECTION 4: That this Resolution shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage
and approval.
